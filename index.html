Privacy Policy

HariyaInfoBot · Last updated: November 25, 2026

HariyaInfoBot ("the App") is an automation tool used by the Facebook Page Hariya Info to publish Sri Lankan news posts to that Page. This policy explains what data the App handles.

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect, store or process personal information from Facebook users, visitors or followers. It does not read comments, messages, profiles or friend lists. The App only uses a Page access token, belonging to the Page owner, to publish posts to the Hariya Info Page.

2. How information is used

The only data the App sends is the content of the post (an image and a text caption) to the Facebook Graph API. The access token is stored as a private secret in the owner's repository and is never shared or published.

3. Third-party services

To prepare posts, the App uses Meta (Facebook Graph API) for publishing and Google Gemini API for writing and classifying news text. News headlines and article text, not personal data, are sent to these services. Their own privacy policies apply to data they process.

4. Data sharing and retention

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. Since the App does not collect user data, there is none to retain.

5. Data deletion

As the App holds no personal data about users, there is nothing to delete. If you believe the App has stored information about you, email us at the address below and we will remove it within 30 days.
